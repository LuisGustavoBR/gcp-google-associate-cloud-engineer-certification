# Personalized Service Health

> **Level:** Advanced · **Topic:** [Operations and cost](../README.md) · **Cited in:** 3 practice-exam questions

## What it is

Reports **Google Cloud** health events (incidents and maintenance) relevant to the project or organization. It shows affected products and locations, updates, and workarounds. It allows creating alerts tied to a Cloud Monitoring notification channel.

## Trigger

"several failures at the same time, with no anomaly in the project's own metrics", which suggests a problem on Google's side.
