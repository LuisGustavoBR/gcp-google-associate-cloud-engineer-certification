# Cloud Hub

> **Level:** Advanced · **Topic:** [Operations and cost](../README.md) · **Cited in:** 2 practice-exam questions

## What it is

**application**-centric view. It brings together health, Cloud Monitoring incidents, and operational information about resources in one place.

## Trigger

"a single console to see the health of the application and its resources".

## Watch out

It serves an organization's *own* applications. Problem events from Google itself are found in Personalized Service Health.
