# Observability Analytics

> **Level:** Intermediate · **Topic:** [Operations and cost](../README.md) · **Cited in:** 2 practice-exam questions

## What it is

New name for **Log Analytics**. It queries logs with **SQL**, in log buckets with analytics enabled. With a linked BigQuery dataset, it joins logs with other data without building an export pipeline.

## Trigger

"query logs with SQL", "without maintaining an export pipeline".
