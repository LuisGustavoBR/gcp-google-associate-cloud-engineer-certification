# Application Design Center

> **Level:** Advanced · **Topic:** [IaC, platform and AI tooling](../README.md) · **Cited in:** 7 practice-exam questions

## What it is

Catalog of approved architecture **templates**. It imports Terraform modules as components. Teams deploy self-service and update through the update/redeploy flow.

## Trigger

"standardize architectures without requiring approval on every deployment", "shared template", "review the architecture before deploying".
