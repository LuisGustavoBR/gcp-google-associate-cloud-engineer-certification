# Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform: Agent Runtime

> **Level:** Advanced · **Topic:** [IaC, platform and AI tooling](../README.md) · **Cited in:** 4 practice-exam questions

## What it is

Managed environment to **deploy, operate, and scale AI agents** in production. It removes the need to manage Kubernetes, VMs, or custom autoscaling.

## Watch out

If the agent consumes Pub/Sub, grant the roles to the service account configured as the agent's identity.
