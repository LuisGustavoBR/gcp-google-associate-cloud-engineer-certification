# Cloud Marketplace

> **Level:** Basic · **Topic:** [IaC, platform and AI tooling](../README.md) · **Cited in:** 3 practice-exam questions

## What it is

Catalog of ready-made software. Deploy directly from the listing, with no installation.

## Trigger

"ready-made CMS or Jenkins" ([E1Q1](../../99-practice-exams/exam-1.md#q1), and [E4Q33](../../99-practice-exams/exam-4.md#q33)). "Managed MongoDB with commercial support" ([E5Q12](../../99-practice-exams/exam-5.md#q12)).
