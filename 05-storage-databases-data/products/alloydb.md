> **Level:** Intermediate · **Topic:** [Storage, databases and data](../README.md) · **Cited in:** 2 practice-exam questions

## What it is

High-performance managed PostgreSQL, for heavy transactional and analytical workloads.

## Trigger

"PostgreSQL with much higher transactional performance" ([E2Q9](../../99-practice-exams/exam-2.md#q9), [E3Q4](../../99-practice-exams/exam-3.md#q4)). Migration is done with **Database Migration Service**.
