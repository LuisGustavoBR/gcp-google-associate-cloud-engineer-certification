# Bigtable

> **Level:** Intermediate · **Topic:** [Storage, databases and data](../README.md) · **Cited in:** 4 practice-exam questions

## What it is

Wide-column NoSQL, with low latency and massive scale.

## Trigger

"telemetry", "time series", "row key with identifier and timestamp" ([E1Q8](../../99-practice-exams/exam-1.md#q8), [E4Q34](../../99-practice-exams/exam-4.md#q34), [E6Q34](../../99-practice-exams/exam-6.md#q34)). The explanation of [E1Q24](../../99-practice-exams/exam-1.md#q24) says the Enterprise Plus edition has an integrated in-memory tier for sub-millisecond reads (taken from the exam, verify in the Bigtable docs).
