> Weights are approximate. A third-party site (SaveMyCert) shows a different layout of 4 domains weighted 20/30/30/20 (with 55 questions in 120 minutes). The official page lists the 5 sections below. Always check the official guide before booking the exam: Google changes the guide from time to time.
