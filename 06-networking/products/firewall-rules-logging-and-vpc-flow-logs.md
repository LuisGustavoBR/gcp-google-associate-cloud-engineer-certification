# Firewall Rules Logging and VPC Flow Logs

> **Level:** Intermediate · **Topic:** [Networking](../README.md) · **Cited in:** 3 practice-exam questions

## What it is

The first shows whether **a specific rule** was triggered. The second records flow metadata and bytes, without packet contents.

## Trigger

"find out whether this deny rule is blocking and which VMs are trying" ([E3Q27](../../99-practice-exams/exam-3.md#q27), [E5Q19](../../99-practice-exams/exam-5.md#q19)). "Understand the cost of inter-region traffic" ([E6Q43](../../99-practice-exams/exam-6.md#q43)).
