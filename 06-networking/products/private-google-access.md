# Private Google Access

> **Level:** Intermediate · **Topic:** [Networking](../README.md) · **Cited in:** 1 practice-exam question

## What it is

Lets VMs with only an internal IP reach Google APIs, enabled per **subnet**.

## Trigger

"no internet access, but needs to read from Cloud Storage" ([E4Q7](../../99-practice-exams/exam-4.md#q7)).

## Watch out

It is not for reaching third-party private services. Private Service Connect and Private services access are other products.
