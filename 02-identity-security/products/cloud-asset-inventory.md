# Cloud Asset Inventory

> **Level:** Intermediate · **Topic:** [Identity and security](../README.md) · **Cited in:** 3 practice-exam questions

## What it is

Searchable inventory of resources and IAM policies across the whole organization.

## Trigger

"find service account keys older than 120 days" (`gcloud asset search-all-resources`, [E3Q15](../../99-practice-exams/exam-3.md#q15), [E5Q17](../../99-practice-exams/exam-5.md#q17)). "VMs with an external IP in the organization" ([E6Q18](../../99-practice-exams/exam-6.md#q18)).
