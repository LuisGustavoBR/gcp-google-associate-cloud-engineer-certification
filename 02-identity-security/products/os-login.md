# OS Login

> **Level:** Intermediate · **Topic:** [Identity and security](../README.md) · **Cited in:** 3 practice-exam questions

## What it is

Manages SSH access centrally through IAM and groups. Can be required by an org policy.

## Trigger

"add and remove engineers automatically, without distributing keys" ([E4Q40](../../99-practice-exams/exam-4.md#q40), [E5Q13](../../99-practice-exams/exam-5.md#q13), [E5Q31](../../99-practice-exams/exam-5.md#q31)).
