# App Engine

> **Level:** Basic · **Topic:** [Compute and containers](../README.md) · **Cited in:** 1 practice-exam question

## What it is

Serverless PaaS organized into **services** and **versions**.

## Trigger

"split 8% of the traffic to the new version".

## Watch out

The split is between **versions of the same service**. Do not create a separate service ([E2Q22](../../99-practice-exams/exam-2.md#q22)).
