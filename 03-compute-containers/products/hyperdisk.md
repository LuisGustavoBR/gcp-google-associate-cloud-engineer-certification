# Hyperdisk

> **Level:** Intermediate · **Topic:** [Compute and containers](../README.md) · **Cited in:** 2 practice-exam questions

## What it is

High-performance disk with provisionable IOPS and throughput. *Extreme* is the one with the highest IOPS.

## Trigger

"SAP HANA above 230 thousand IOPS", "database installed on a VM with a fast disk" ([E2Q44](../../99-practice-exams/exam-2.md#q44), [E3Q45](../../99-practice-exams/exam-3.md#q45)).
